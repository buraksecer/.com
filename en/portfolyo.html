Papara

2023 – 2024 · Istanbul

I joined a team of two and took ownership of a next-generation ATM, from industrial design through software and system architecture. I started by studying ATM regulations and wrote my first code on the physical device. The team grew to five or six people.

We connected the C++ core library through a proxy DLL. The HTML interface exchanged messages with the desktop app over a socket. A .NET Core middleware service communicated with Papara services while the ATM polled a database for management commands. I designed locking so the device processed one operation at a time.

I designed CI/CD for the physical device. A separate controller waited for the active operation to finish, entered maintenance mode, unpacked the release in a separate directory, swapped application files and restarted the ATM. We had a working demo around month six. The device went live within a year and attracted a buyer at Money20/20 in the Netherlands.

What does Burak do day to day?

  • Develop ATM software and service integrations.
  • Test withdrawal and maintenance flows on the physical device.
  • Verify locking and CI/CD work together.
